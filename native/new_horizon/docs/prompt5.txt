Read @docs/DESCRIPTION.md for context.
Then look at the sun shadow calculation in @moonlib/pipeline/LightmapPipeline.cs .
This code generates geotiffs that represent the amount of sunlight at each pixel in a DEM at given times.
Currently, the geotiffs being generated are wrong in two ways.
1. The sun does not appear to be at the correct azimuth for the given times when compared with older code that has been validated.
2. Too many of the images show full sun in every pixel, which is not possible for locations at the latitude of the DEM.  I don't know whether the sun's elevation is wrong, or the horizon is wrong or the lighting calculation is wrong or something else.
Pay attention to GenerateShadows on line 103 of @moonlib/pipeline/LightmapPipeline.cs.  This method fills 128 x 128 pixel tiles in geotiff tiles with the amount of sun at each pixel in the underlying DEM using the BuilderSunFraction method of @moonlib/LightmapGenerator.cs .
Also look at GenerateMatrices on line 86, which calls the GetMatrix method of @moonlib/ElevationMap.cs .  This code, which generates a 4 x 4 rotation and translation matrix, is from the old code which had a different convention for the azimuth, and this matrix may also be wrong.
